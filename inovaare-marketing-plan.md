## Executive Summary

Inovaare is an AI-powered healthcare payer compliance platform serving Medicare, Medicaid, and commercial health plans. Founded in 2006 and Gartner-recognized, Inovaare reduces audit preparation time by up to 90% through configurable automation of Appeals & Grievances (A&G), compliance monitoring, and delegate oversight.

This marketing plan targets a **12-month growth horizon** aimed at increasing pipeline velocity, expanding brand authority in the healthcare compliance space, and growing ARR from ~$5.2M toward a $10M+ milestone through a combination of content authority, paid acquisition, event presence, and partner channels.

### 1.3 Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Primary Buyer:**
- **Title:** VP/Director of Compliance, Chief Compliance Officer, VP of Operations
- **Organization:** Medicare Advantage, Managed Medicaid, and Commercial health plans (500–50,000 members)
- **Pain Points:**
  - CMS audit preparation consuming excessive staff hours
  - Manual A&G processes causing regulatory risk and SLA failures
  - Fragmented compliance data across departments
  - Inability to demonstrate audit readiness in real time

**Secondary Buyer/Influencer:**
- **Title:** CIO, VP of IT, Director of Healthcare Operations
- **Focus:** Integration with EHRs/claims systems, API-first requirements, security/compliance (HIPAA, HITRUST)

**Firmographic Targets:**
- Health plans with 100,000–2M members
- Organizations facing CMS star rating pressure
- Plans expanding from fee-for-service to value-based contracts
- Organizations post-merger integrating compliance systems

*Hub: "Appeals & Grievances for Health Plans: The Definitive Guide"*

#### Pillar 3: AI in Healthcare Payer Operations
*Hub: "AI-Powered Compliance: A Health Plan Leader's Guide"*
