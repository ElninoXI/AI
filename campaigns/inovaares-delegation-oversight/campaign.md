**Approach**:

## Sending Notes

- Send from individual sales rep email addresses, not a marketing alias
- Avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons — best: Tue–Thu, 8–10am recipient local time
- Plain-text or minimal HTML only — outreach emails should feel personal, not designed
- Unsubscribe link required in footer (CAN-SPAM / GDPR compliance)
- BCC or log to CRM on each send for reply tracking
